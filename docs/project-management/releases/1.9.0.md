# Release 1.9.0

HiveLog's positive-state colour (`ok`) follows the Beeswax palette, and a faint honeycomb sits behind HiveLog, account and sign-in pages.

## Highlights
- `--hivelog-ok` and `--hivelog-ok-tint` are mapped from new `--bw-ok` and `--bw-ok-tint` tokens, beside critical and warning.
- `--bw-ok` is a yellower green than the pine accent, so a status stays distinct from the brand colour on a tile with accent links. Contrast is at least 5.15:1 in light and 7.5:1 in dark.
- A faint honeycomb background, as in the Vinculum iOS app: pointy-top hexagon outlines in the accent colour at 10%, fading out downwards, behind HiveLog pages, account pages and sign-in cards. Pure CSS; tune or turn off with `--bw-honeycomb-*` tokens.
- Needs the HiveLog release containing the `ok` stat-tile variant (HiveLog task 0256); on an older HiveLog the tokens are unused.
